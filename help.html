How to Raise a Query via the Paytm App?

Follow these simple steps to quickly address your concerns through the Paytm application:

  1. Open the Paytm App

    Ensure you're logged into your account. The app is your gateway to accessing a wide range of services and support.

  2. Navigate to 'Help Support'

    Tap on your profile picture or use the search bar to find the 'Help Support' section. This is your starting point for any queries.

  3. Select the Service You Need Help With

    You'll see tiles for different services such as Banking, Recharges, Bill Payments, and More. Choose the one related to your issue.

  4. Submit Your Query

    After selecting the relevant service, choose the specific order or transaction you have an issue with. An assistant bot will guide you to submit your query or issue effectively.

That's it! By following these steps, you can easily raise a query and get the assistance you need from the Paytm app.

FAQs

  1. How can you keep your Paytm account safe?

    Use strong, unique passwords for your Paytm account and keep your mobile number and email address updated. Don't share your password or OTP with anyone. Use secure internet connections and enable 2FA for added security. Log out of shared devices and keep your phone and app updated. Be cautious of phishing and fraud attempts.

  2. How can I resolve issues with failed UPI transactions?

    If UPI transactions are failing, check internet connection, verify details, check balance, check transaction limit, contact bank, try again later, check with UPI provider. Keep the bank and UPI app teams informed for further investigation.

  3. Money transferred through Paytm UPI but the receiver did not get it?

    Majority of UPI payments get successful instantly and money is added to the receiver's bank account at the moment itself. However, in rare circumstances which are beyond our control, like not receiving a success message from NPCI or receiver bank within a stipulated time, transactions go into pending state For pending cases, we keep checking the status with NPCI on regular basis and within 24 hours, we know the final status — success or failure of all payments. For failed payments, all banks do a reconciliation and add the money back to your bank account within 2 working days.
